# Godot vertical slice

This is the new production prototype for **Where the Stars Remember**.

## Open it
1. Open Godot 4.x.
2. Click **Import**.
3. Choose this folder's `project.godot` file.
4. Open the project and press **F6/F5** to run.

## Current milestone
- Compact single-planet structure rather than an open world.
- Third-person camera placed higher and aimed down toward the character.
- Correct camera-relative WASD controls: A = left, D = right.
- Brunette placeholder heroine with teddy on her shoulder.
- One seed shrine.
- Press E beside the seed to awaken the planet.
- Flowers emerge progressively across the world.
- Flowers sway and bend when the player passes them.
- Birds orbit the planet and rise away when approached.

The primitive meshes are intentional scaffolding. The next art pass replaces them with a unified miniature-diorama style, custom character silhouettes, richer terrain, vegetation, animation, VFX and a real puzzle for Planet I — The Silent Garden.
